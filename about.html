I was born in Raiganj, a small town in northern West Bengal. But I lived in Kolkata from the time I was six months old till I came to the US for pursuing my Ph.D at UC Santa Cruz in 2020. I attended Kendriya Vidyalaya No-2 Salt Lake from standard 1 till 12. I graduated with my doctoral degree (Ph.D) in Astronomy from University of California, Santa Cruz. I pursued my bachelors and masters in Physics from Presidency University, Kolkata.

I love music. I can play a variety of Indian percussion instruments like Tabla, Pakhwaj, Dholak, Naal and Bhangra dhol. I have accompanied many singers and groups with these instruments. I love Indian instrumental classical music. Sitar and Sharod recitals are my favorite. I love acting in plays. I have acted in several plays with various kinds of roles. I am completely detached from acting now with all the busy academic schedules but I am up for it anytime I will get a chance.

I got selected in IISc for integrated Phd through JAM. I was also pre-selected for PhD at IUCAA, Pune through INAT. I have a perfect score of 990 at Physics GRE, 2018. I was a S.N. Bose Scholar and visited UCSC for the summer of 2019.
